# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Current and prospective members of Kooperatiba para sa Kaunlaran ng Lagonoy. They use the site to get official information and the right printable form before they visit the main office in Lagonoy or the branch in Caramoan.

## Product Purpose

KKL’s public website explains the cooperative and hosts its requirement forms. A visit succeeds when someone can tell what KKL is, which service applies, which PDF to print, and which office to bring it to. Membership, savings, loans, and other transactions are completed at the counter, not on the site.

## Positioning

This is the cooperative’s own public record: its registered name, history, services as the cooperative describes them, published events, and the actual requirement PDFs. It is not an online application, a member portal, or a place that states terms the board has not published here.

## Operating Context

People print a form, fill it in, and submit it at the office. The main office is at F. Pamor St., San Isidro Sur, Lagonoy, Camarines Sur, with the consumer store beside it. The branch is in Caramoan, Camarines Sur, opened in June 2015. Bring a valid ID with any downloaded form. New members take the Pre-Membership Education Seminar. Share capital amounts, loan approval, and any extra documents follow the current policies of the Board of Directors. The site does not publish a phone number or email; inquiries are in person.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a public information site. Pages are Home, About, Services, Events, Forms, and Contact.

Services described on the site: share capital, savings through the Member Savers Club (launched in 2001), credit assistance for members in good standing, the consumer store, and member education.

Forms, printed and filed at the office:

- Membership Form (`public/forms/MEMBERSHIP FORM.pdf`)
- Member Profile Information (`public/forms/Member Profile Information.pdf`)
- Deposit Application Form (`public/forms/Deposit Application Form.pdf`)
- Loan Application Form (`public/forms/LOAN APPLICATION FORM.pdf`), noted as back-to-back copies (5 copies)
- Re-subscription Agreement (`public/forms/RE-SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT.pdf`)

The interface stays in English. Filipino remains where it is the cooperative’s own name, theme, or document title.

Do not add online applications, payments, or a member login unless that decision is reopened. Do not invent interest rates, loan amounts, phone numbers, email addresses, or testimonials. Do not replace the official name, CDA Reg. No. NG-743, the two office locations, the published history, the officers named on the 38th anniversary program, or the five PDF forms.

The registered name was Kooperatiba ng mga Kawani ng Lagonoy, Inc. until a 2007 amendment. First registration was with the Bureau of Agricultural Cooperative Development under RV-FF223 on June 1, 1989. CDA registration is September 25, 1991, Reg. No. NG-743. The first members organized after a Pre-Membership Education Seminar on August 26, 1988.

## Product Principles

- Prepare the office visit. The site helps a member or applicant arrive with the right form; the counter completes the transaction.
- Members first. Write for someone who will file at Lagonoy or Caramoan.
- Official record only. Use the published name, registration, addresses, history, officers, events, and PDFs. Leave unpublished terms unpublished.
- English for the interface. Keep Filipino for the cooperative’s own name, themes, and document titles.
- Board policy stays at the office. Point people to current policy instead of stating amounts or approvals the site does not have.
